# Product — CONTEXT

Last updated: 2026-07-21

## What happens here

Define the barber problems the desktop app solves, shape feature behavior, and
record roadmap choices and acceptance criteria before implementation begins.

## Audience and quality bar

- Audience: barbers, shop owners, suite owners, educators, and the team building the product.
- Good work: starts with a real content workflow, names the user-visible outcome,
  covers unhappy paths, and ends with testable acceptance criteria.
- Avoid: feature ideas detached from barber work, invented customer evidence,
  implementation details presented as product requirements, and silent scope expansion.

## Process

1. Check `notes.md` and the current application before describing existing behavior.
2. Write the smallest useful brief in `briefs/`; record durable choices in `decisions/`.
3. Define acceptance criteria and route approved implementation work to `/engineering`.

## Files here

- `notes.md` — confirmed product truth and open questions.
- `briefs/` — feature briefs, workflows, and acceptance criteria.
- `decisions/` — approved choices and their reasoning.
- `research/` — sanitized observations and source-backed product research.

## Rules

- Distinguish proposed, built, tested, released, and externally available.
- Do not store customer contact information, credentials, or private content.
- Ask before changing outward-facing promises, pricing, or publishing behavior.
