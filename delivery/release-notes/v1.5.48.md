# 6FB Content Studio v1.5.48

## New: Thumbnail Maker

- Adds a dedicated Thumbnail Maker for turning a selected Content Studio video
  into three distinct, transcript-grounded YouTube thumbnail directions.
- Keeps the creator in control: titles, headlines, visual treatment, accent,
  and proof cues are editable before generating or exporting a cover.
- Produces polished 16:9 PNG thumbnails from a video frame plus an editorial
  barbering-art direction, with the final headline rendered by the app for
  reliable, exact text.

## Local library and privacy

- Saves completed thumbnail packages locally so a creator can reopen, refine,
  and export work without repeating a generation request.
- Validates saved records and image paths before they are reopened; finished
  covers remain in app-owned local storage unless the creator exports them.
- Image and package generation are user-initiated and use bounded requests.
  Thumbnail Maker does not upload video, publish content, schedule posts, or
  modify social accounts.

## Certification

This release targets **macOS arm64 only**. The tag workflow stages exactly the
DMG, updater ZIP, ZIP blockmap, and `latest-mac.yml` as a private draft and
verifies the notarized DMG before it can be reviewed. A separate,
owner-approved promotion follows authenticated Mac acceptance and verifies the
anonymous public assets. Windows distribution remains deferred pending measured
Mac adoption.
