# 6FB Content Studio v1.5.47

## New: Smart Live Trends

- Adds **Find live trends** to Video Planner. On an explicit request, it can
  surface current Google Trends signals, eligible connected Instagram
  professional-account signals, and clearly labelled topics from your 6FB
  Content Planner.
- Keeps broad signals honest: each result identifies whether it is Live,
  Cached, Your plan, or an Idea starter, and barber-fit filtering leads with
  useful starters when a current broad topic is not relevant to your saved
  Content Brain.
- Adds an optional, consent-based YouTube inspiration section supplied through
  the signed-in 6FB service. YouTube results are reference-only: they open their
  original source and never become a planner topic or affect barber-fit ranking.
- Removes the unavailable TikTok trend source. The app does not scrape TikTok,
  Instagram, or YouTube.

## Privacy and controls

- Live retrieval runs only when you press **Find live trends**; it does not
  poll, post, schedule, or change connected social accounts.
- YouTube discovery requires current consent and a signed-in 6FB account. You
  can disable it in Settings at any time, which clears its local consent and
  cached references.
- Settings links to 6FB Privacy, 6FB Terms, YouTube Terms, and Google Privacy.

## Certification

This release targets **macOS arm64 only**. The tag workflow stages exactly the
DMG, updater ZIP, ZIP blockmap, and `latest-mac.yml` as a private draft and
verifies the notarized DMG before it can be reviewed. A separate,
owner-approved promotion follows authenticated Mac acceptance and verifies the
anonymous public assets. Windows distribution remains deferred pending measured
Mac adoption.
