# 6FB Content Studio v1.5.50

## Improved Settings

- Puts your 6FB account and connection status at the top of Settings, where
  they are easiest to find and act on.
- Keeps Content Planner, Instagram, and YouTube inspiration controls together
  in the everyday workflow rather than mixing them with device diagnostics.
- Makes **Sign in with 6FB** the clear browser-based connection path while
  retaining password sign-in as an available fallback.
- Moves API keys, runtime health, storage, app details, and reset controls into
  an accessible Advanced section without removing them.

## Certification

This release targets **macOS arm64 only**. The tag workflow stages exactly the
DMG, updater ZIP, ZIP blockmap, and `latest-mac.yml` as a private draft and
verifies the notarized DMG before publication. Windows distribution remains
deferred pending measured Mac adoption.
