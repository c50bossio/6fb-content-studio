# 6FB Content Studio v1.5.54

## More relevant Instagram account trends

- Keeps low-fit authorized Instagram account activity out of Smart Trends so
  unrelated personal or broad lifestyle posts do not become content ideas.
- Retains direct barber-domain Instagram signals, even when their numeric
  barber-fit score is below the general live-signal threshold.
- Keeps live-source status truthful when Instagram returns account activity but
  none of it qualifies as a useful barber trend.

## Certification

This release targets **macOS arm64 only**. The tag workflow stages exactly the
DMG, updater ZIP, ZIP blockmap, and `latest-mac.yml` as a private draft and
verifies the notarized DMG before any separately approved public promotion.
Windows distribution remains deferred pending measured Mac adoption.
