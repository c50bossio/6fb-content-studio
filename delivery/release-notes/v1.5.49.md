# 6FB Content Studio v1.5.49

## New: Sign in with 6FB in your browser

- Adds a browser-based 6FB sign-in option in Settings, so you can connect the
  Mac app through your existing 6FB account without entering that password in
  the desktop app.
- Keeps password sign-in available as a fallback.
- Returns you to Content Studio after sign-in and shows the connected account
  in Settings.

## Safer account handoff

- Uses a one-time, short-lived browser handoff with PKCE protection.
- Keeps browser cookies and access tokens out of app URLs and out of the
  renderer process.
- Validates the local callback before the app exchanges its one-time code with
  the 6FB Content service.

## Certification

This release targets **macOS arm64 only**. The tag workflow stages exactly the
DMG, updater ZIP, ZIP blockmap, and `latest-mac.yml` as a private draft and
verifies the notarized DMG before publication. Windows distribution remains
deferred pending measured Mac adoption.
