# 6FB Content Studio v1.5.53

## More relevant Google Trends ideas

- Keeps broad, unrelated Google Trends headlines out of the Content Studio
  topic picker when they do not meet the barber-fit threshold.
- Retains clearly barber-specific Google headlines, even when a single direct
  barber-domain term is the only matching signal.
- Preserves authorized Instagram account signals and 6FB Content Planner topics
  alongside qualifying Google Trends ideas.

## Certification

This release targets **macOS arm64 only**. The tag workflow stages exactly the
DMG, updater ZIP, ZIP blockmap, and `latest-mac.yml` as a private draft and
verifies the notarized DMG before publication. Windows distribution remains
deferred pending measured Mac adoption.
